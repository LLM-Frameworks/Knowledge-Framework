## Shared rules for every seat

- Label every block with your seat name and phase
- Tag factual claims: [Certain] [Likely] [Theory] [Guessing]
- Do not quietly rewrite the defeat/success condition; propose a formal revision instead
- Court-style: Discovery is written first; new evidence after Discovery only if Moderator/Steward allows

- Output is a proposal until the Steward says otherwise
