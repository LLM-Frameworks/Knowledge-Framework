# Blog framework — A Stone's Throw

## Name

**A Stone's Throw.**

- Challenge the status quo; put a stone in the shoe (Koukl), not a stone at the person.
- “He who is without sin should cast the first stone — and that’s not my job.”
- The frameworks are building stones; the name is both the challenge and the material.

## Editorial standard

Nothing publishes unless it has survived:

- Every atomic claim tiered ([Certain] / [Likely] / [Theory] / [Guessing]) with receipt (source + warrant).
- The other side steel-manned (“Did I get that right?”) before answering.
- Memory-sourced claims capped at [Likely]; admitted in prose when published (“from memory,” “as I recall”).
- Steward red-pen completed; nothing bypasses it.

## The loop

1. **Fuel** — Briefing across the lanes; holiday watch when relevant.
2. **Capture** — Seeds in `Settings/seeds.md` (by topic). Half-formed welcome.
3. **Draft** — Author drafts in his voice (`Settings/drafts/` while in progress).
4. **Red-pen** — Sparring loop with any AI as equal partner; Steward decides.
5. **Publish** — One canonical store (GitHub library); other surfaces are views (Substack, etc.). Never edit only in the views.

## Relationship to Core

This app does not invent a second evidence system. It uses Core tags, receipts, and authority rules. Project-specific requirements live in `requirements.md`.
